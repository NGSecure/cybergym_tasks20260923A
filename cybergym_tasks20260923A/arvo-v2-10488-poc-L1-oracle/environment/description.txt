In Skia (MSan), clipping a canvas against a path whose point coordinates were edited in place after the path's bounding box had already been computed and cached leaves that cached box stale, so when the anti-aliased fill rasterizer later lays out its per-scanline coverage segments it sizes its working storage from the now-too-small cached box instead of the path's real, larger extent, and emitting a segment for geometry that falls outside that stale box writes past the end of the storage (out-of-bounds write).
